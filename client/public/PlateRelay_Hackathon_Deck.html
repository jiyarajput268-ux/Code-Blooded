🔴 PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Urban Food Paradox: Waste vs. Hunger

🚮

67 Million Tons Wasted

India loses ₹92,000 Crores worth of edible food annually. Commercial kitchens, mess halls, and bakeries dump fresh surplus daily.

⏳

The Perishability Clock

Cooked food spoils within 2–4 hours. Traditional phone-call networks to NGOs fail because food expires before volunteers arrive.

🚫

No Unified Logistics

Zero real-time platforms exist to automatically match commercial donors with nearby verified shelters, langars, and volunteer runners.

💡 PROPOSED SOLUTION

PlateRelay: Real-Time 3-Stage Cascade System

STAGE 1

Rescue Deal (50-70% Off)

Initial commercial resale window allowing donors to recover food costs while food is super fresh.

STAGE 2

Free Share Feed

Zero-cost community sharing feed activated automatically as the safe consumption window decreases.

STAGE 3

NGO Bulk Rescue

Automated direct routing to verified shelters and langars for bulk portions (≥ 15 portions).

⏱️ Safety Countdown Clock Ring: Displays live safe-for timer (e.g. 3h 45m) ensuring zero spoiled food distribution.
✨ KEY FEATURES & INNOVATION

Intelligent Modules & Value Proposition

🧠 Automated Weighted NGO Matcher

Calculates dynamic match scores (78/100) using Distance (40%) + Capacity (25%) + Urgency (20%) + Preference (15%).

📷 Smart Dish Matcher & Photo Picker

Auto-detects dish titles (Momos, Paneer, Biryani, Chole Bhature, Gulab Jamun, Dal Makhani) with direct device file upload support.

🔒 Dual-OTP Handoff Security

Pickup OTP (#4821) and Delivery OTP (#9374) verify volunteer runner chain-of-custody to eliminate fraud.

🗺️ OpenStreetMap Leaflet Integration

Live interactive map with custom Leaflet markers for Temples, Langars, and Verified Community Shelters.
